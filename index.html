Do I need my phone for AI features?

Connected AI and translation use the companion app, a compatible phone connection and supported services. Availability can vary by app version and region.

How does the pre-order work?

Message KTECH with your selected model. Once payment details are confirmed, reserve with a 50% down payment and pay the remaining 50% before delivery.

Contact KTECH to confirm the included accessories for your chosen model before reserving. The APEX setup guide covers using the supplied charger and caring for your glasses.

What about warranty, returns and delivery fees?
