SUPPORT / APEX

Start here.
Then look up.

English instructions adapted from the supplied APEX manual. These button controls apply to APEX; NOVA and NEO may differ.

Connect your glasses.

  1. Install WAKEOK

    Search for WAKEOK in your phone’s app store, or use the download QR code in the original manual.

  2. Turn on Bluetooth and APEX

    Enable Bluetooth on your phone. Hold the right button on your glasses for 3 seconds and release after the startup sound.

  3. Pair in the app

    Open WAKEOK, search for devices and select your glasses’ Bluetooth name to connect and bind them.

Use your voice.

The supplied manual lists the English wake phrase “Hey Fans”. The wake phrase may vary with firmware. You can also activate AI with one press of the left button.

  • “Take a picture” — capture a photo.
  • “Take a video” — begin video recording.
  • “Take a record” — begin audio recording, as worded in the manual.
  • “Take the call” — answer a call.
  • “Increase the volume” or “Reduce the volume”.

Save, translate and update.

Bring photos and recordings onto your phone

Use the app’s album for photos and videos, and the audio section for recordings. The manual lists USB or the device’s local-network transfer for importing media; follow the transfer prompts in your app. Imported files can be shared from the app.

Translation and meeting tools

Select a supported translation mode and your languages in the app. The manual describes simultaneous interpretation, one-click translation, face-to-face translation and meeting voice/text capture. Availability depends on the app version and supported services.

Firmware updates and device finding

Connect the glasses to WAKEOK to check for firmware updates. Keep the battery charged and the network stable throughout an update. Use the app’s Find Device function to help locate your glasses.

Lights and care.

The capture light flashes once for a photo and every five seconds during video recording. The manual lists a flashing red charging light, a blue fully-charged light and five red flashes for low battery.

Use the supplied charger and keep the charging contacts clean and dry. Never charge in a wet environment. Wipe lenses with a soft cloth, avoid chemical solvents and store the glasses in their case away from heat, moisture and direct sunlight.

The manual lists automatic shutdown after five minutes without use. Settings and behaviour may vary by firmware. Contact KTECH if your controls or app differ.
